Important Abbreviations

AI - Artificial Intelligence
Technology that analyzes farm data and provides smart irrigation recommendations.
IoT - Internet of Things
Network of smart devices and sensors used for monitoring farms.
GIS - Geographic Information System
System used for mapping and analyzing agricultural land.
GPS - Global Positioning System
Satellite-based navigation system used for locating farms and equipment.
ET - Evapotranspiration
The combined process of water evaporation and plant transpiration used for irrigation planning.
