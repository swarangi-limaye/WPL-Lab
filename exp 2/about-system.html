~OBJECTIVES~

  • Develop an AI-enabled irrigation advisory system for sugarcane cultivation that integrates field observations, weather information, and crop-specific requirements to support informed irrigation decision-making.
  • Monitor soil moisture, environmental conditions, and crop status using sensor networks and geospatial technologies to enable plot-level assessment and precision irrigation management.
  • Generate real-time, forecast-based, and plot-specific irrigation and fertigation recommendations to optimize irrigation scheduling, improve resource utilization, and support operational constraints such as electricity availability.
  • Develop farmer-friendly web and mobile platforms for advisory dissemination, visualization of geofenced plots, crop information management, and decision support.
  • Enhance water use efficiency, reduce yield losses due to water stress, and improve the sustainability and climate resilience of sugarcane farming practices.

~SYSTEM FEATURES~

  • IoT System for data acquisition
  • AI-enabled irrigation advisory platform
  • Farmer dashboard
  • Multi-source integrated agricultural data repository
  • Real-time irrigation recommendation system
  • Forecasting and alert mechanisms
  • LLM-based multilingual advisory module
