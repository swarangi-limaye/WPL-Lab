SMART AGRICULTURE

Welcome to Smart Irrigation

The Smart Irrigation Advisory System is an AI-enabled web portal that assists sugarcane farmers in making informed irrigation decisions using soil moisture, weather forecasts, IoT sensors, and AI-generated recommendations.

💧 SMART WATER MANAGEMENT Make every drop count with data-driven irrigation.

Core Objectives

The system focuses on efficient water use, better crop management and sustainable farming.

01

Optimize Water Use

Saves water by only irrigating when necessary.

02

Maximize Sugarcane Yield

Prevents root rot from over-watering and stunting from under-watering.

03

Reduce Energy Costs

Minimizes electricity bills by running water pumps efficiently.

04

Automate Manual Planning

Eliminates guesswork by replacing manual checks with data-driven scheduling.
