IMPORTANT IRRIGATION ADVISORY

Follow these recommendations to improve irrigation efficiency and conserve water.

IRRIGATION RECOMMENDATIONS

Evapotranspiration (ET) & Moisture-Based Scheduling

  • Calculate Net Water Requirement: Match irrigation volume to crop water loss (ETc).
  • Maintain the 50% Depletion Limit: Do not allow Management Allowed Depletion (MAD) to exceed 50% of available water capacity.
  • Monitor Soil Tension:
  1. Coarse/Sandy Soil: 20–30 kPa
  2. Medium/Loam Soil: 40–50 kPa
  3. Heavy/Clay Soil: 50–70 kPa

WATER CONSERVATION

  1. Use drip irrigation or micro-sprinklers to reduce water loss.
  2. Schedule irrigation based on soil moisture and crop needs.
  3. Avoid overwatering and runoff by monitoring soil moisture levels.
  4. Smart irrigation delivers water precisely when and where crops need it most.
  5. Drip irrigation applies water directly to plant roots, reducing evaporation and wind drift.
  6. High-efficiency Drip Irrigation (DI) systems can deliver water directly to the plant base and reduce unnecessary water loss.
